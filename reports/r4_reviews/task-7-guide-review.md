## Scope and remaining evidence

Guide geometry, positive retention, custom connections, stop shoulders, endpoint pins, swept collision checks and reaction-path graphs are design work possible now. They are not external blockers. Ordinary joint fasteners may be proposed explicitly as custom hardware envelopes with grade/torque unresolved; do not invent supplier mounting patterns. A continuous fused bridge is a legitimate custom part candidate but manufacturing access/process must be documented.

External evidence still needed for manufacturing/operating release: real shuttle dimensions and variation, friction/contact behavior, material/fastener strength and tolerances, measured manual loads, actual table anchorage and bench stability, real endpoint pin fit and operator access, prototype repeated cycling. None of these prevents nominal geometry closure, but none is proved by zero B-rep intersections. Positive contact and intended engagement must be distinguished from hard interference; inserted locks are checked only at locked endpoints.
